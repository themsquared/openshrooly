How we make money from links.

Last updated October 25, 2026.

Some links on Shroom Labs are affiliate links. If you buy through one, we may earn a commission. It doesn't cost you anything extra.

Where you'll see them

  • Only on culinary and functional Profiles, like oyster, shiitake, or reishi.
  • Never on Off-menu Profiles. No affiliate links, paid items, or Grow Coach there.
  • Never inside Profiles other growers post. We strip affiliate links out of community Profiles.

How we pick them

We link to supplies that fit the grow: substrate, grow bags, spawn, and tools. A commission never changes a Profile's settings. If a cheaper option works just as well, we'll say so.
