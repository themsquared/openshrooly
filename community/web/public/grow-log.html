What each entry records

  • Date (YYYY-MM-DD)
  • Chamber
  • Grow day
  • Stage
  • Temperature (°C, from the chamber log)
  • Humidity (%, from the chamber log)
  • What we saw
  • Harvest weight (g, scale, only on harvest days)

Readings come from the chamber's own log. Harvest weights come from a kitchen scale. Blank means not measured.

FAQ

When does the grow log start?

When the Lykyn arrives and we confirm we can read and set it from outside. The two Shroolys start the same day so the comparison is fair.

Are the numbers real?

Yes. Each entry comes from a chamber log or a kitchen scale. If we did not measure it, the entry leaves it blank.

Why compare a Shrooly and a Lykyn?

Both are small home chambers. Growing the same species from the same blocks in each shows how much the chamber itself changes the result.
