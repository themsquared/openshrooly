FAQ

Answers, then the chamber.

How do I apply a Profile to a chamber?

Browse a culinary Profile, pull the JSON to your Hub on the LAN, then apply a stage. The Hub talks to OpenShrooly. No cloud is required to grow.

What is the Hub?

Hub is the local app that stores Profiles and sends setpoints to a chamber. It runs on a computer on your network.

What is Grow Coach?

Grow Coach is a $3.99 per month culinary subscription. It learns one chamber from telemetry and past flushes, then suggests safer humidity and temperature.

Does this work with a Shrooly device?

Yes. Works with Shrooly devices running OpenShrooly. Shroom Labs is the profile library, not the hardware brand.

Do I need the cloud?

No. Growing is local-first. Claim a device only if you want optional cloud tools. Local controls still win if the cloud is down.

How long does pearl oyster take?

Pearl Oyster is about 29 days from start to fruit at cool, wet setpoints with high fresh air once pins form.

Why do lion's mane spines turn yellow?

Too much fresh air or low humidity. Lion's Mane wants moderate FAE, high RH, and cool fruiting.

What humidity does shiitake want?

Shiitake fruits cooler and a bit drier than oyster. Keep the block moist, not dripping, after the browning rest.

Where are Golden Teacher and other Psilocybe climate recipes?

They are Off-menu Profiles, fully indexed on this site. Psilocybin mushrooms are illegal to grow, possess or sell in most countries and in most US states under federal law. This information is published for educational, research and harm-reduction purposes only. Check the laws where you live. Shroom Labs does not sell spores, cultures or psilocybin products.

Culinary Profiles

  • Beech / Buna Shimeji: Beech / Buna Shimeji fruits at 15 C and 90% RH with moderate FAE and 10 h light. About 40 days from start to fruit. Clustered beech mushroom. Cool, humid, and a little less FAE than oysters.
  • Blue Oyster: Blue Oyster fruits at 14 C and 87% RH with high FAE and 12 h light. About 25 days from start to fruit. Cool-preferring oyster. Deep color holds better below 18 °C.
  • Button / Cremini: Button / Cremini fruits at 16 C and 88% RH with moderate FAE and 2 h light. About 36 days from start to fruit. Compost + casing species. Low light, cool fruiting, casing required.
  • Chestnut Mushroom: Chestnut Mushroom fruits at 15 C and 92% RH with moderate FAE and 12 h light. About 34 days from start to fruit. Clustered woodlover. Cool fruiting and high RH keep caps glossy.
  • Cordyceps militaris: Cordyceps militaris fruits at 18 C and 84% RH with moderate FAE and 12 h light. About 57 days from start to fruit. Grown on supplemented grain/rice, not logs. Wants 12/12 light once orange initials show.
  • Elm Oyster: Elm Oyster fruits at 15 C and 86% RH with moderate FAE and 12 h light. About 31 days from start to fruit. Not a true Pleurotus. Cooler fruiting, meaty single fruits more than oyster clusters.
  • Enoki: Enoki fruits at 15 C and 93% RH with low FAE and 4 h light. About 43 days from start to fruit. Needs cold fruiting and low FAE for supermarket-long stems.
  • Golden Oyster: Golden Oyster fruits at 21 C and 87% RH with high FAE and 12 h light. About 24 days from start to fruit. Warm oyster with dense golden clusters. Needs strong FAE to keep stems short.
  • King Oyster: King Oyster fruits at 15 C and 82% RH with moderate FAE and 10 h light. About 38 days from start to fruit. Thick-stem oyster. Cooler, slightly drier, and less FAE than cluster oysters.
  • King Oyster — thick-stem method: King Oyster — thick-stem method fruits at 14 C and 80% RH with low FAE and 8 h light. About 43 days from start to fruit. Paid example recipe: cooler, drier fruiting to favor thick stems.
  • Lion's Mane: Lion's Mane fruits at 17 C and 90% RH with moderate FAE and 10 h light. About 33 days from start to fruit. Tooth fungus. Moderate FAE — too much air yellows spines.
  • Maitake: Maitake fruits at 16 C and 88% RH with moderate FAE and 10 h light. About 54 days from start to fruit. Slow woodlover. Primordia look like brains; do not dry them out.
  • Nameko: Nameko fruits at 14 C and 93% RH with moderate FAE and 10 h light. About 39 days from start to fruit. Cool, very wet fruiting. The slime coat is normal.
  • Pearl Oyster: Pearl Oyster fruits at 16 C and 88% RH with moderate FAE and 12 h light. About 29 days from start to fruit. Forgiving beginner oyster. Fast, high FAE, cool-to-room fruiting.
  • Pink Oyster: Pink Oyster fruits at 24 C and 86% RH with high FAE and 12 h light. About 20 days from start to fruit. Tropical oyster. Wants warmth; stalls and fades in a cold room.
  • Pioppino: Pioppino fruits at 16 C and 86% RH with moderate FAE and 12 h light. About 31 days from start to fruit. Black poplar mushroom. Cool fruiting; keep the surface less wet than oysters.
  • Reishi: Reishi fruits at 23 C and 85% RH with moderate FAE and 12 h light. About 77 days from start to fruit. Antlers under high CO2, then consoles when FAE increases.
  • Shiitake: Shiitake fruits at 16 C and 85% RH with moderate FAE and 12 h light. About 74 days from start to fruit. Needs a full colonize + browning rest, then a cold shock before fruiting.
  • Turkey Tail: Turkey Tail fruits at 20 C and 86% RH with moderate FAE and 12 h light. About 78 days from start to fruit. Thin polypore. Long fruiting, lots of FAE, not a thick-fleshed edible.
  • Wine Cap: Wine Cap fruits at 17 C and 85% RH with moderate FAE and 12 h light. About 40 days from start to fruit. Best as an outdoor bed mushroom. Indoor profile is a climate approximation only.
  • Wood Ear: Wood Ear fruits at 24 C and 92% RH with moderate FAE and 10 h light. About 38 days from start to fruit. Warm, wet jelly fungus. High RH matters more than light intensity.

Off-menu Profiles

  • B+: B+ fruits at 21 C and 89% RH with moderate FAE and 12 h light. About 32 days from start to fruit. Forgiving cubensis strain with a slightly wider temperature window.
  • Golden Teacher: Golden Teacher fruits at 22 C and 90% RH with moderate FAE and 12 h light. About 32 days from start to fruit. Common cubensis strain. Standard monotub-style climate mapped onto Shrooly setpoints.
  • Penis Envy: Penis Envy fruits at 22 C and 90% RH with moderate FAE and 12 h light. About 50 days from start to fruit. Slower cubensis. Same climate band as Golden Teacher with longer stage timers.
  • Psilocybe cyanescens: Psilocybe cyanescens fruits at 15 C and 90% RH with moderate FAE and 10 h light. About 52 days from start to fruit. Cool-weather woodlover. Indoor chambers are a poor substitute for outdoor beds.
  • Psilocybe natalensis: Psilocybe natalensis fruits at 23 C and 90% RH with moderate FAE and 12 h light. About 29 days from start to fruit. Cubensis-adjacent climate; often described as a bit more vigorous.
