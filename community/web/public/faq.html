Questions

What is a profile?

A researched humidity, light, and air recipe you can apply to a chamber running OpenShrooly.

Do I have to use the cloud?

No. Claiming a chamber is optional. Local controls still work if the network is down.

What is Grow Coach?

A culinary subscription that learns your chamber and suggests safer setpoints. It is not sold with Off-menu profiles.
