FAQ

Answers, then the chamber.

How do I apply a Profile to a chamber?

Pick a culinary Profile, pull it to your Hub on your home network, then apply a stage. The Hub talks to OpenShrooly. No cloud is required to grow.

What is the Hub?

Hub is the local app that stores Profiles and sends setpoints to a chamber. It runs on a computer on your network.

What is Grow Coach?

Grow Coach is a $3.99 per month culinary subscription. It learns one chamber from its readings and past flushes, then suggests safer humidity and temperature.

Does this work with a Shrooly device?

Yes. Works with Shrooly devices running OpenShrooly. Shroom Labs is the profile library, not the hardware brand.

Do I need the cloud?

No. Growing runs on your own network. Claim a device only if you want optional cloud tools. Local controls still win if the cloud is down.

How long does pearl oyster take?

Pearl Oyster is about 29 days from start to fruit at cool, wet setpoints with high fresh air once pins form.

Why do lion's mane spines turn yellow?

Too much fresh air or low humidity. Lion's Mane wants moderate FAE, high RH, and cool fruiting.

What humidity does shiitake want?

Shiitake fruits cooler and a bit drier than oyster. Keep the block moist, not dripping, after the browning rest.

Where are Golden Teacher and other Psilocybe climate recipes?

They are Off-menu Profiles, fully indexed on this site. Psilocybin mushrooms are illegal to grow, possess, or sell in most countries and under US federal law. This information is published for educational, research and harm-reduction purposes only. Check the laws where you live. Shroom Labs does not sell spores, cultures or psilocybin products.
