FAQ

Answers, then the chamber.

How do I apply a profile?

Browse a culinary card, pull the JSON to a hub on your LAN, and apply a stage to an OpenShrooly chamber. No cloud is required to grow.

What is Grow Coach?

A $3.99/month culinary subscription that learns one chamber from telemetry. It is not sold with off-menu varieties.

Where are the other species?

A few chamber recipes stay off the printed menu. They are educational setpoints, indexed for search, and hidden from the default catalog until you opt in.
