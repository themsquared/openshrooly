How it works

01

Browse a Profile

Pick a culinary variety. Each card is humidity, temperature, FAE, light, and days. Not a shop SKU.

02

Pull it to your Hub

Hub is the local app on your network. It stores Profiles. No cloud is required to grow.

03

Apply a stage

Hub talks to OpenShrooly over the LAN. You watch the flush at home.
