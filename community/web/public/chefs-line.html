Common questions

What is the Chef's line?

Paid grow profiles that aim at a specific result in the kitchen, such as a denser head, a thicker stem, a cracked flower cap, or a deeper color. Each one runs on a Shrooly chamber with OpenShrooly and is backed by published sources.

Do I keep a profile after I buy it?

Yes. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. The full recipe unlocks on your account and stays there. You can find it under Purchases on your account page.

What do I get when I buy one?

Every stage with its temperature, humidity, fresh air, and light, the stage notes, the light recipe, and the sources behind them. Your hub can pull it and apply it to your chamber.

Are the photos from these profiles?

No. They are licensed photos of the same species, credited under each one. They show the mushroom, not a harvest from this profile.
