Optional cloud link

Link your chamber to your account

Link your chamber to your Shroom Labs account so you can see it, get grow alerts by email, and change its profile from anywhere.

Cloud linking needs a firmware update that is not out yet. Until then your chamber works fully on your own network. Open its page on your Wi-Fi, or use Hub to apply Profiles.

What you need

  • A chamber running OpenShrooly on your Wi-Fi
  • A Shroom Labs account with a verified email
  • The next firmware update, which is not out yet

How pairing works

Arriving with the next firmware update. Cloud stays off until you turn it on.

  1. Chamber
  2. Code
  3. This page
  4. Account

  1. 01

    Turn it on

    On your chamber's local web page, turn on Connect to Shroom Labs. Cloud is off unless you do.

  2. 02

    Read the code

    The chamber shows a 6-character code and a QR on its screen and local web page. The code lasts 15 minutes.

  3. 03

    Scan or type

    Scan the QR with your phone, or come to this page signed in and type the code.

  4. 04

    Linked

    The chamber is linked to your account and keeps its own security certificate renewed automatically. No password on the device.

Selling or replacing a chamber? Remove it from your account and the new owner pairs it again.
