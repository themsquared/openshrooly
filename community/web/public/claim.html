How linking works
  1. On the chamber, press button 4 to open the menu and choose Link this device. On OpenShrooly older than 1.0.6-sl.1, turn on Connect to Shroom Labs on the chamber's own page instead. It stays off unless you do.
  2. The chamber shows a 6-character code and a QR code.
  3. Scan the QR with your phone, or type the code here while signed in.
  4. Done. Your chamber shows up in your account and you can change what it grows from anywhere. There is no password on the chamber.

Linking is optional. It gives you your chamber on your phone, email alerts, and remote profile changes. Selling or replacing a chamber? Remove it from your account and the new owner links it again.
