Common questions

What temperature do mushrooms grow at?

Fruiting temperatures in this library run from 14 °C (57 °F) to 24 °C (75 °F), depending on the species. Colonization usually runs a few degrees warmer than fruiting.

What humidity do mushrooms need to fruit?

Between 82% and 93% while fruiting for the species here. Most sit near 90%.

Which mushrooms grow fastest?

Pink Oyster (about 20 days), Golden Oyster (about 24 days), Blue Oyster (about 25 days) are the quickest profiles in this library, from the start of colonization to the end of the first harvest window.

What is the easiest mushroom to grow at home?

Oyster mushrooms. Blue Oyster, Pearl Oyster, Pink Oyster are tagged beginner here because they colonize fast and forgive small mistakes.
