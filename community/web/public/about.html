About Mike Moore and Shroom Labs

Last updated Nov 17, 2026.

I'm Mike Moore. I run Shroom Labs, a small independent project that keeps Shrooly mushroom chambers growing and publishes culinary grow guides and profiles with cited numbers.

What I do here

I publish the guides, grow profiles and blog posts on this site and check each number against the sources listed on the page. I also build, test and sign the free OpenShrooly firmware releases. Shroom Labs builds on OpenShrooly, the open source firmware started by Graham Stewart (grahamsz).

How I test

Everything runs on my own chambers first: two Shroolys and a Lykyn Smart Mushroom Grow Box, plus a fruiting chamber I built from an ESP32 and ESPHome, which I wrote up in the blog. The grow log shows what is running and what has been measured so far.

What I cover

I grow and write about culinary mushrooms. The Off-menu reference pages list published conditions for research and education only. They are not cultivation instructions, and you are responsible for following the laws where you live.

Independence

Shroom Labs is not made by or affiliated with the company behind Shrooly.

Corrections and contact

If a number is wrong or a page is out of date, tell me and I will fix it. Use the contact form. A real person reads every message.

You can also find me on X and GitHub.
