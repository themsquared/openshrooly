Culinary Profile

Nameko

Pholiota microspora, Nameko

Nameko fruits at 14 °C and 93% RH with moderate FAE and 10 h light. About 39 days from start to fruit.

Cool, very wet fruiting. The slime coat is normal.

Grow parameters

  1. Colonization 20 days, 22 °C, 90% RH
  2. Pinning 7 days, 15 °C, 95% RH
  3. Fruiting 12 days, 14 °C, 93% RH
