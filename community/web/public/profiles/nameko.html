Culinary Profile

Nameko

Nameko fruits at 14 C and 93% RH with moderate FAE and 10 h light. About 39 days from start to fruit. Cool, very wet fruiting. The slime coat is normal.

Pholiota microspora, Nameko

Grow parameters

  1. Colonization 20 d, 90% RH, 22 C

  2. Pinning 7 d, 95% RH, 15 C

  3. Fruiting 12 d, 93% RH, 14 C
