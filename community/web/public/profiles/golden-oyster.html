Culinary Profile

Golden Oyster

Golden Oyster fruits at 21 C and 87% RH with high FAE and 12 h light. About 24 days from start to fruit. Warm oyster with dense golden clusters. Needs strong FAE to keep stems short.

Pleurotus citrinopileatus, Golden

Grow parameters

  1. Colonization 12 d, 90% RH, 25 C

  2. Pinning 4 d, 93% RH, 22 C

  3. Fruiting 8 d, 87% RH, 21 C

    More light helps hold gold color.
