How to grow Golden Oyster at home

Pleurotus citrinopileatus, Golden

Updated 2026-10-26

To grow Golden Oyster at home, colonize at 25 °C (77 °F) in the dark, then fruit at 21 °C (70 °F) and 87% humidity with plenty of fresh air and 12 h of light a day. This profile runs about 24 days from the start of colonization to the end of the first harvest window.

Warm oyster with dense golden clusters. Needs plenty of fresh air to keep stems short.

Light recipe

Dark while it colonizes, then 12 h a day of cool white with some blue.

Light details

P. citrinopileatus has no variety-specific lux in the sources below. The recipe is the P. ostreatus oyster default and is labeled as such.

WS2812 blue is roughly 465 to 475 nm. The RGB mix is an approximation of cited wavelengths, not a measured spectrum.

What goes wrong when growing Golden Oyster?

No pins after colonization

Make sure the block is fully colonized first. Then check that the chamber reaches the pinning conditions: 22 °C (72 °F), 93% humidity, and moderate fresh air. Pins need more fresh air than colonization. More on this.

Long, thin stems and small caps

This usually means too little fresh air. This profile runs plenty of fresh air: fan runs 40 s every 10 min while fruiting. More on this.

Dry, cracked, or curled caps

Usually humidity is too low, or air is blowing straight on the mushrooms. This profile holds 87% humidity while fruiting. More on this.

Green, black, or slimy patches

Patches that are not white mycelium are usually contamination such as green mold or bacteria. Bag the block, take it out of the chamber, and clean the chamber before the next grow. More on this.

Golden Oyster growing FAQ

What temperature does Golden Oyster grow at?

In this profile Golden Oyster colonizes at 25 °C (77 °F) and fruits at 21 °C (70 °F). Colonization: 25 °C (77 °F). Pinning: 22 °C (72 °F). Fruiting: 21 °C (70 °F).

What humidity does Golden Oyster need?

87% while fruiting. Colonization: 90%. Pinning: 93%. Fruiting: 87%.

How long does Golden Oyster take to grow?

About 24 days with this profile: 12 days of colonization, 4 days of pinning, 8 days of fruiting. Real blocks vary, so move on when the block is ready, not only when the days run out.

How much light does Golden Oyster need?

Colonization: dark. Pinning: 12 h a day, white 20%, blue 80%. Fruiting: 12 h a day, white 20%, blue 80%.

How much fresh air does Golden Oyster need?

Plenty of fresh air: fan runs 40 s every 10 min while fruiting. Colonization runs with little fresh air.
