Culinary Profile

King Oyster . thick-stem method

King Oyster . thick-stem method fruits at 14 C and 80% RH with low FAE and 8 h light. About 43 days from start to fruit. Paid example recipe: cooler, drier fruiting to favor thick stems.

Pleurotus eryngii, King

Grow parameters

  1. Colonization 20 d, 88% RH, 21 C

  2. Pinning 7 d, 88% RH, 15 C

    Fewer, larger pins beat a full flush of thin fruits.

  3. Fruiting 16 d, 80% RH, 14 C
