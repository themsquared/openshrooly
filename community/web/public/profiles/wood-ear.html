Culinary Profile

Wood Ear

Wood Ear fruits at 24 C and 92% RH with moderate FAE and 10 h light. About 38 days from start to fruit. Warm, wet jelly fungus. High RH matters more than light intensity.

Auricularia auricula-judae, Black wood ear

Grow parameters

  1. Colonization 18 d, 90% RH, 26 C

  2. Pinning 6 d, 94% RH, 24 C

  3. Fruiting 14 d, 92% RH, 24 C
