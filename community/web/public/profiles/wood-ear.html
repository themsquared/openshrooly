How to grow Wood Ear at home

Auricularia auricula-judae, Black wood ear

Updated 2026-10-26

To grow Wood Ear at home, colonize at 26 °C (79 °F) in the dark, then fruit at 24 °C (75 °F) and 92% humidity with moderate fresh air and 12 h of light a day. This profile runs about 38 days from the start of colonization to the end of the first harvest window.

Warm, wet jelly fungus. High humidity matters more than light intensity.

Light recipe

Dark while it colonizes, then 12 h a day of cool white with some blue.

Light details

No variety-specific light numbers were found. Colonization is dark. Fruiting uses the general default: 12 h, 20% white (92 lux on this chamber), and 80% blue as a WS2812 stand-in for 440 to 470 nm.

WS2812 blue is roughly 465 to 475 nm. The RGB mix is an approximation of cited wavelengths, not a measured spectrum.

What goes wrong when growing Wood Ear?

No pins after colonization

Make sure the block is fully colonized first. Then check that the chamber reaches the pinning conditions: 24 °C (75 °F), 94% humidity, and moderate fresh air. Pins need more fresh air than colonization. More on this.

Green, black, or slimy patches

Patches that are not white mycelium are usually contamination such as green mold or bacteria. Bag the block, take it out of the chamber, and clean the chamber before the next grow. More on this.

Wood Ear growing FAQ

What temperature does Wood Ear grow at?

In this profile Wood Ear colonizes at 26 °C (79 °F) and fruits at 24 °C (75 °F). Colonization: 26 °C (79 °F). Pinning: 24 °C (75 °F). Fruiting: 24 °C (75 °F).

What humidity does Wood Ear need?

92% while fruiting. Colonization: 90%. Pinning: 94%. Fruiting: 92%.

How long does Wood Ear take to grow?

About 38 days with this profile: 18 days of colonization, 6 days of pinning, 14 days of fruiting. Real blocks vary, so move on when the block is ready, not only when the days run out.

How much light does Wood Ear need?

Colonization: dark. Pinning: 12 h a day, white 20%, blue 80%. Fruiting: 12 h a day, white 20%, blue 80%.

How much fresh air does Wood Ear need?

Moderate fresh air: fan runs 25 s every 16 min while fruiting. Colonization runs with little fresh air.
