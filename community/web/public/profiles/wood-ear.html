Culinary Profile

Wood Ear

Auricularia auricula-judae, Black wood ear

Wood Ear fruits at 24 °C and 92% RH with moderate FAE and 10 h light. About 38 days from start to fruit.

Warm, wet jelly fungus. High RH matters more than light intensity.

Grow parameters

  1. Colonization 18 days, 26 °C, 90% RH
  2. Pinning 6 days, 24 °C, 94% RH
  3. Fruiting 14 days, 24 °C, 92% RH
