Culinary Profile

Wine Cap

Stropharia rugosoannulata, Garden giant

Wine Cap fruits at 17 °C and 85% RH with moderate FAE and 12 h light. About 40 days from start to fruit.

Best as an outdoor bed mushroom. Indoor profile is a climate approximation only.

Grow parameters

  1. Colonization 21 days, 22 °C, 85% RH
  2. Pinning 7 days, 18 °C, 90% RH
  3. Fruiting 12 days, 17 °C, 85% RH
