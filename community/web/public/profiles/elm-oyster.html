Culinary Profile

Elm Oyster

Elm Oyster fruits at 15 C and 86% RH with moderate FAE and 12 h light. About 31 days from start to fruit. Not a true Pleurotus. Cooler fruiting, meaty single fruits more than oyster clusters.

Hypsizygus ulmarius, Elm

Grow parameters

  1. Colonization 16 d, 88% RH, 22 C

  2. Pinning 5 d, 92% RH, 16 C

  3. Fruiting 10 d, 86% RH, 15 C
