Educational setpoints

Golden Teacher

Golden Teacher fruits at 22 C and 90% RH with moderate FAE and 12 h light. About 32 days from start to fruit. Common cubensis strain. Standard monotub-style climate mapped onto Shrooly setpoints.

Psilocybe cubensis, Golden Teacher

Psilocybin mushrooms are illegal to grow, possess or sell in most countries and in most US states under federal law. This information is published for educational, research and harm-reduction purposes only. Check the laws where you live. Shroom Labs does not sell spores, cultures or psilocybin products.

Grow parameters

  1. Colonization 16 d, 92% RH, 25 C

    Dark, still. Do not introduce FAE early.

  2. Pinning 6 d, 95% RH, 22 C

  3. Fruiting 10 d, 90% RH, 22 C
