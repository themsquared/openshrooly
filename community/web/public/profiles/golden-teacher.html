Golden Teacher

Psilocybe cubensis, Golden Teacher

Legality of cultivating Psilocybe species varies by jurisdiction. This pack is informational environmental setpoints for a fruiting chamber and is disabled by default. You are responsible for following local law.

Golden Teacher fruits at 22 °C and 90% RH with moderate FAE and 12 h light. About 32 days from start to fruit.

Common cubensis strain. Standard monotub-style climate mapped onto Shrooly setpoints.

Grow parameters

  1. Colonization 16 days, 25 °C, 92% RH

    Dark, still. Do not introduce FAE early.

  2. Pinning 6 days, 22 °C, 95% RH
  3. Fruiting 10 days, 22 °C, 90% RH
