Educational setpoints

B+

B+ fruits at 21 C and 89% RH with moderate FAE and 12 h light. About 32 days from start to fruit. Forgiving cubensis strain with a slightly wider temperature window.

Psilocybe cubensis, B+

Legality of cultivating Psilocybe species varies by jurisdiction. This pack is informational environmental setpoints for a fruiting chamber and is disabled by default. You are responsible for following local law.

Grow parameters

  1. Colonization 16 d, 92% RH, 24 C

  2. Pinning 6 d, 95% RH, 21 C

  3. Fruiting 10 d, 89% RH, 21 C

Works with Shrooly devices running OpenShrooly.
