Psilocybe cubensis, B+

Legality of cultivating Psilocybe species varies by jurisdiction. This pack is informational environmental setpoints for a fruiting chamber and is disabled by default. You are responsible for following local law.

B+ fruits at 21 °C and 89% RH with moderate FAE and 12 h light. About 32 days from start to fruit.

Forgiving cubensis strain with a slightly wider temperature window.

Grow parameters

  1. Colonization 16 days, 24 °C, 92% RH
  2. Pinning 6 days, 21 °C, 95% RH
  3. Fruiting 10 days, 21 °C, 89% RH
