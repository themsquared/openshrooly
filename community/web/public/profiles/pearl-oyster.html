Culinary Profile

Pearl Oyster

Pearl Oyster fruits at 16 C and 88% RH with moderate FAE and 12 h light. About 29 days from start to fruit. Forgiving beginner oyster. Fast, high FAE, cool-to-room fruiting.

Pleurotus ostreatus, Pearl

Grow parameters

  1. Colonization 14 d, 90% RH, 24 C

    Dark, low FAE until the block is white.

  2. Pinning 5 d, 93% RH, 18 C

    Drop temperature and open FAE to trigger clusters.

  3. Fruiting 10 d, 88% RH, 16 C

    Harvest when caps flatten and edges just begin to upturn.
