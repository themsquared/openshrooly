Culinary Profile

Pearl Oyster

Pleurotus ostreatus, Pearl

Pearl Oyster fruits at 16 °C and 88% humidity with moderate fresh air and 12 h light. About 29 days from start to fruit.

Forgiving beginner oyster. Fast, lots of fresh air, cool-to-room fruiting.

Grow parameters

  1. Colonization 14 days, 24 °C, 90% humidity

    Dark, little fresh air until the block is white.

  2. Pinning 5 days, 18 °C, 93% humidity

    Drop temperature and add fresh air to trigger clusters.

  3. Fruiting 10 days, 16 °C, 88% humidity

    Harvest when caps flatten and edges just begin to upturn.
