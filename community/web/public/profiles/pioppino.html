How to grow Pioppino at home

Cyclocybe aegerita, Black poplar

Updated 2026-10-27

Pioppino (Cyclocybe aegerita), also called black poplar mushroom or Agrocybe aegerita, takes about 31 days to grow at home, from spawn to the end of the first harvest. Colonize at 25 °C (77 °F) in the dark, then fruit at 16 °C (61 °F) and 86% humidity with moderate fresh air and 12 h of light a day.

Black poplar mushroom. Cool fruiting; keep the surface less wet than oysters.

Light recipe

Dark while it colonizes, then 12 h a day of cool white with some blue.

Light details

No variety-specific light numbers were found. Colonization is dark. Fruiting uses the general default: 12 h, 20% white (92 lux on this chamber), and 80% blue as a WS2812 stand-in for 440 to 470 nm.

WS2812 blue is roughly 465 to 475 nm. The RGB mix is an approximation of cited wavelengths, not a measured spectrum.

What goes wrong when growing Pioppino?

No pins after colonization

Make sure the block is fully colonized first. Then check that the chamber reaches the pinning conditions: 17 °C (63 °F), 92% humidity, and moderate fresh air. Pins need more fresh air than colonization. More on this.

Dry, cracked, or curled caps

Usually humidity is too low, or air is blowing straight on the mushrooms. This profile holds 86% humidity while fruiting. More on this.

Green, black, or slimy patches

Patches that are not white mycelium are usually contamination such as green mold or bacteria. Bag the block, take it out of the chamber, and clean the chamber before the next grow. More on this.

Kit or block doing nothing? Work through grow kit not growing. To check your fresh air, put your chamber size and fan into the fresh air planner.

Pioppino growing FAQ

What temperature does Pioppino grow at?

In this profile Pioppino colonizes at 25 °C (77 °F) and fruits at 16 °C (61 °F). Colonization: 25 °C (77 °F). Pinning: 17 °C (63 °F). Fruiting: 16 °C (61 °F).

What humidity does Pioppino need?

86% while fruiting. Colonization: 88%. Pinning: 92%. Fruiting: 86%.

How long does Pioppino take to grow?

About 31 days with this profile: 16 days of colonization, 5 days of pinning, 10 days of fruiting. Real blocks vary, so move on when the block is ready, not only when the days run out.

How much light does Pioppino need?

Colonization: dark. Pinning: 12 h a day, white 20%, blue 80%. Fruiting: 12 h a day, white 20%, blue 80%.

How much fresh air does Pioppino need?

Moderate fresh air: fan runs 30 s every 15 min while fruiting. Colonization runs with little fresh air.
