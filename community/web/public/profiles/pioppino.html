Culinary Profile

Pioppino

Pioppino fruits at 16 C and 86% RH with moderate FAE and 12 h light. About 31 days from start to fruit. Black poplar mushroom. Cool fruiting; keep the surface less wet than oysters.

Cyclocybe aegerita, Black poplar

Grow parameters

  1. Colonization 16 d, 88% RH, 25 C

  2. Pinning 5 d, 92% RH, 17 C

  3. Fruiting 10 d, 86% RH, 16 C

    Ease RH so caps stay dry enough to open cleanly.
