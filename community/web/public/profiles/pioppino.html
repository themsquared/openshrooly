Culinary Profile

Pioppino

Cyclocybe aegerita, Black poplar

Pioppino fruits at 16 °C and 86% humidity with moderate fresh air and 12 h light. About 31 days from start to fruit.

Black poplar mushroom. Cool fruiting; keep the surface less wet than oysters.

Grow parameters

  1. Colonization 16 days, 25 °C, 88% humidity
  2. Pinning 5 days, 17 °C, 92% humidity
  3. Fruiting 10 days, 16 °C, 86% humidity

    Ease humidity so caps stay dry enough to open cleanly.
