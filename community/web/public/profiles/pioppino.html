Pioppino

Black poplar mushroom. Cool fruiting; keep the surface less wet than oysters.

Cyclocybe aegerita · Black poplar

Grow parameters

  1. Colonization 16 d · 88% RH · 25 °C

  2. Pinning 5 d · 92% RH · 17 °C

  3. Fruiting 10 d · 86% RH · 16 °C

    Ease RH so caps stay dry enough to open cleanly.
