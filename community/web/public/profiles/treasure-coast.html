Treasure Coast

Psilocybe cubensis, Treasure Coast

Psilocybin mushrooms are illegal to grow, possess, or sell in most countries and under US federal law. This information is published for educational, research and harm-reduction purposes only. Check the laws where you live. Shroom Labs does not sell spores, cultures or psilocybin products.

Treasure Coast fruits at 22 °C and 90% humidity with moderate fresh air and 12 h light. About 32 days from start to fruit.

Cubensis strain named for the Treasure Coast of Florida, where it is said to have been collected. Same climate as other cubensis strains.

Grow parameters

  1. Colonization 16 days, 25 °C, 92% humidity

    Dark, still. Do not add fresh air early.

  2. Pinning 6 days, 22 °C, 95% humidity
  3. Fruiting 10 days, 22 °C, 90% humidity

Light recipe

Dark while it colonizes, then 12 h a day of cool white with some blue.

Light details

P. cubensis needs light to start pins, and blue light works for that. Colonization is dark. Pinning and fruiting get 12 hours a day at 20% white (92 lux on this chamber) with 80% blue.

WS2812 blue is roughly 465 to 475 nm. The RGB mix is an approximation, not a measured spectrum.
