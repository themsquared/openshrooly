How to grow Cordyceps militaris at home

Cordyceps militaris, Cultivated militaris

Updated 2026-10-26

To grow Cordyceps militaris at home, colonize at 20 °C (68 °F) in the dark, then fruit at 18 °C (64 °F) and 84% humidity with moderate fresh air and 12 h of light a day. This profile runs about 57 days from the start of colonization to the end of the first harvest window.

Grown on supplemented grain/rice, not logs. Wants 12/12 light once orange initials show.

How long does Cordyceps militaris take to grow?

About 57 days from the start of colonization to the end of the first harvest window.

  1. Day 1 to 12: Colonization

    Dark. Do not start light until the surface is colonized.

  2. Day 13 to 22: Orange initials

    Bright, cooler light helps stroma form.

  3. Day 23 to 57: Stroma

Light recipe

Dark while it colonizes, then 12 h a day of cool white with some blue.

Light details

Colonization stays dark. Fruiting uses 12 h and 51% white, mapped from 1250 lux (cordycepin optimum in Wu 2016; 1750 lux gave more dry weight). RGB is blue-heavy from later LED work on compound levels, not from a yield result or a measured spectrum on this strip.

WS2812 blue is roughly 465 to 475 nm. The RGB mix is an approximation of cited wavelengths, not a measured spectrum.

What goes wrong when growing Cordyceps militaris?

What does this profile watch for?

This profile only sets climate. Media (brown rice + silkworm-pupa or vegan analog) is on you. Keep jars or trays in the chamber.

No pins after colonization

Make sure the block is fully colonized first. Then check that the chamber reaches the orange initials conditions: 18 °C (64 °F), 86% humidity, and moderate fresh air. Pins need more fresh air than colonization. More on this.

Green, black, or slimy patches

Patches that are not white mycelium are usually contamination such as green mold or bacteria. Bag the block, take it out of the chamber, and clean the chamber before the next grow. More on this.

Cordyceps militaris growing FAQ

What temperature does Cordyceps militaris grow at?

In this profile Cordyceps militaris colonizes at 20 °C (68 °F) and fruits at 18 °C (64 °F). Colonization: 20 °C (68 °F). Orange initials: 18 °C (64 °F). Stroma: 18 °C (64 °F).

What humidity does Cordyceps militaris need?

84% while fruiting. Colonization: 82%. Orange initials: 86%. Stroma: 84%.

How long does Cordyceps militaris take to grow?

About 57 days with this profile: 12 days of colonization, 10 days of orange initials, 35 days of stroma. Real blocks vary, so move on when the block is ready, not only when the days run out.

How much light does Cordyceps militaris need?

Colonization: dark. Orange initials: 12 h a day, white 51%, blue 80%. Stroma: 12 h a day, white 51%, blue 80%.

How much fresh air does Cordyceps militaris need?

Moderate fresh air: fan runs 20 s every 25 min while fruiting. Colonization runs with little fresh air.

Is Cordyceps militaris good for beginners?

It is one of the harder species in this library. Try an oyster first if you have never grown before.
