Culinary Profile

Cordyceps militaris

Cordyceps militaris, Cultivated militaris

Cordyceps militaris fruits at 18 °C and 84% humidity with moderate fresh air and 12 h light. About 57 days from start to fruit.

Grown on supplemented grain/rice, not logs. Wants 12/12 light once orange initials show.

Grow parameters

  1. Colonization 12 days, 20 °C, 82% humidity

    Dark. Do not start light until the surface is colonized.

  2. Orange initials 10 days, 18 °C, 86% humidity

    Bright, cooler light helps stroma form.

  3. Stroma 35 days, 18 °C, 84% humidity

Light recipe

Colonization stays dark. Fruiting uses 12 h and 51% white, mapped from 1250 lux (cordycepin optimum in Wu 2016; 1750 lux gave more dry weight). RGB is blue-heavy from later LED trials, not from a measured spectrum on this strip.

WS2812 blue is roughly 465 to 475 nm. The RGB mix is an approximation of cited wavelengths, not a measured spectrum.

Recipe sources

  • Wu 2016 Cordyceps militaris. Chiu-Yeh Wu et al., Effects of Illumination Pattern during Cultivation of Fruiting Body and Bioactive Compound Production by Cordyceps militaris, Int. J. Med. Mushrooms 18 (2016). Colonization in a spawn room without a stated photoperiod. Primordia and fruiting at 12 h per day. Highest dry yield at 1750 plus or minus 250 lux. Highest cordycepin at 1250 plus or minus 250 lux. This recipe maps 51% white to about 1250 lux.
  • Chang 2024 Cordyceps LED. Single-wavelength LED work on Cordyceps militaris found glare-blue wavelengths raised fruiting-body weight and cordycepin versus other colors (Journal of Food Biochemistry style LED trial, 2024). Used here only for the blue bias, not for a lux number.
