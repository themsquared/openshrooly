Culinary Profile

Cordyceps militaris

Cordyceps militaris fruits at 18 C and 84% RH with moderate FAE and 12 h light. About 57 days from start to fruit. Grown on supplemented grain/rice, not logs. Wants 12/12 light once orange initials show.

Cordyceps militaris, Cultivated militaris

Grow parameters

  1. Colonization 12 d, 82% RH, 20 C

    Dark. Do not start light until the surface is colonized.

  2. Orange initials 10 d, 86% RH, 18 C

    Bright, cooler light helps stroma form.

  3. Stroma 35 d, 84% RH, 18 C
