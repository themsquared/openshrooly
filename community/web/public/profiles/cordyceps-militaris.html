Culinary Profile

Cordyceps militaris

Cordyceps militaris, Cultivated militaris

Cordyceps militaris fruits at 18 °C and 84% RH with moderate FAE and 12 h light. About 57 days from start to fruit.

Grown on supplemented grain/rice, not logs. Wants 12/12 light once orange initials show.

Grow parameters

  1. Colonization 12 days, 20 °C, 82% RH

    Dark. Do not start light until the surface is colonized.

  2. Orange initials 10 days, 18 °C, 86% RH

    Bright, cooler light helps stroma form.

  3. Stroma 35 days, 18 °C, 84% RH
