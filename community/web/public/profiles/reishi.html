Culinary Profile

Reishi

Ganoderma lucidum, Red reishi

Reishi fruits at 23 °C and 85% humidity with moderate fresh air and 12 h light. About 77 days from start to fruit.

Antlers under high CO2, then consoles when fresh air increases.

Grow parameters

  1. Colonization 21 days, 27 °C, 88% humidity
  2. Antler / primordia 21 days, 24 °C, 90% humidity

    Little fresh air keeps growth as antlers.

  3. Console formation 35 days, 23 °C, 85% humidity

    Increase fresh air and light to flatten antlers into consoles.

Light recipe

Colonization is dark. Fruiting uses 12 h and 37% white, mapped from 500 lux in the Corvinus literature table (200 to 500 lux early, 500 to 1000 lux later). Blue is the general photomorphogenesis mix. A 2024 paper found green raised weight and red delayed caps; we do not invent a green-heavy mix from that.

WS2812 blue is roughly 465 to 475 nm. The RGB mix is an approximation of cited wavelengths, not a measured spectrum.

Recipe sources

  • Corvinus University reishi thesis. Petra Maszlaver, Cultivation possibilities for production of reishi mushroom Ganoderma lucidum, Corvinus University of Budapest PhD (2011). Literature table: 200 to 500 lux in early fruiting, 500 to 1000 lux later. 500 lux maps to 37% white.
  • Agriculture 2024 Ganoderma light. Study on the Effects of Different Light Supply Modes on Ganoderma lucidum, Agriculture 14 (2024). 12 h / 12 h at 15 umol/m2/s. Red delayed or blocked differentiation. Green raised fruit weight. Blue is still used here as the general photomorphogenesis mix; this paper did not give a lux number for our white channel.
  • Oregon State Extension reishi. OSU Extension EM 9364, Reishi Mushroom Growing Guide. Antler versus shelf form is tied to oxygen, not a lux number. Colonize blocks before opening them to fruiting.
  • Stamets. Paul Stamets, Growing Gourmet and Medicinal Mushrooms, Ten Speed Press. Used here as a general cultivation reference. This recipe does not take a photoperiod or lux number from a cited page in that book.
