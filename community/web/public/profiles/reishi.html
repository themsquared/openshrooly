Culinary Profile

Reishi

Ganoderma lucidum, Red reishi

Reishi fruits at 23 °C and 85% RH with moderate FAE and 12 h light. About 77 days from start to fruit.

Antlers under high CO2, then consoles when FAE increases.

Grow parameters

  1. Colonization 21 days, 27 °C, 88% RH
  2. Antler / primordia 21 days, 24 °C, 90% RH

    Low FAE keeps growth as antlers.

  3. Console formation 35 days, 23 °C, 85% RH

    Increase FAE and light to flatten antlers into consoles.
