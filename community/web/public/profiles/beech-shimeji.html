Culinary Profile

Beech / Buna Shimeji

Beech / Buna Shimeji fruits at 15 C and 90% RH with moderate FAE and 10 h light. About 40 days from start to fruit. Clustered beech mushroom. Cool, humid, and a little less FAE than oysters.

Hypsizygus tessulatus, Brown beech

Grow parameters

  1. Colonization 21 d, 88% RH, 22 C

  2. Pinning 7 d, 94% RH, 16 C

  3. Fruiting 12 d, 90% RH, 15 C
