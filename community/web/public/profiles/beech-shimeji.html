How to grow Beech / Buna Shimeji at home

Hypsizygus tessulatus, Brown beech

Updated 2026-10-26

To grow Beech / Buna Shimeji at home, colonize at 22 °C (72 °F) in the dark, then fruit at 15 °C (59 °F) and 90% humidity with moderate fresh air and 12 h of light a day. This profile runs about 40 days from the start of colonization to the end of the first harvest window.

Clustered beech mushroom. Cool, humid, and a little less fresh air than oysters.

Light recipe

Dark while it colonizes, then 12 h a day of cool white with some blue.

Light details

No variety-specific light numbers were found. Colonization is dark. Fruiting uses the general default: 12 h, 20% white (92 lux on this chamber), and 80% blue as a WS2812 stand-in for 440 to 470 nm.

WS2812 blue is roughly 465 to 475 nm. The RGB mix is an approximation of cited wavelengths, not a measured spectrum.

What goes wrong when growing Beech / Buna Shimeji?

No pins after colonization

Make sure the block is fully colonized first. Then check that the chamber reaches the pinning conditions: 16 °C (61 °F), 94% humidity, and moderate fresh air. Pins need more fresh air than colonization.

Dry, cracked, or curled caps

Usually humidity is too low, or air is blowing straight on the mushrooms. This profile holds 90% humidity while fruiting.

Green, black, or slimy patches

Patches that are not white mycelium are usually contamination such as green mold or bacteria. Bag the block, take it out of the chamber, and clean the chamber before the next grow.

Beech / Buna Shimeji growing FAQ

What temperature does Beech / Buna Shimeji grow at?

In this profile Beech / Buna Shimeji colonizes at 22 °C (72 °F) and fruits at 15 °C (59 °F). Colonization: 22 °C (72 °F). Pinning: 16 °C (61 °F). Fruiting: 15 °C (59 °F).

What humidity does Beech / Buna Shimeji need?

90% while fruiting. Colonization: 88%. Pinning: 94%. Fruiting: 90%.

How long does Beech / Buna Shimeji take to grow?

About 40 days with this profile: 21 days of colonization, 7 days of pinning, 12 days of fruiting. Real blocks vary, so move on when the block is ready, not only when the days run out.

How much light does Beech / Buna Shimeji need?

Colonization: dark. Pinning: 12 h a day, white 20%, blue 80%. Fruiting: 12 h a day, white 20%, blue 80%.

How much fresh air does Beech / Buna Shimeji need?

Moderate fresh air: fan runs 25 s every 18 min while fruiting. Colonization runs with little fresh air.
