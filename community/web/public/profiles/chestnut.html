Culinary Profile

Chestnut Mushroom

Chestnut Mushroom fruits at 15 C and 92% RH with moderate FAE and 12 h light. About 34 days from start to fruit. Clustered woodlover. Cool fruiting and high RH keep caps glossy.

Pholiota adiposa, Chestnut

Grow parameters

  1. Colonization 18 d, 90% RH, 23 C

  2. Pinning 6 d, 95% RH, 16 C

  3. Fruiting 10 d, 92% RH, 15 C
