Culinary Profile

Chestnut Mushroom

Pholiota adiposa, Chestnut

Chestnut Mushroom fruits at 15 °C and 92% humidity with moderate fresh air and 12 h light. About 34 days from start to fruit.

Clustered woodlover. Cool fruiting and high humidity keep caps glossy.

Grow parameters

  1. Colonization 18 days, 23 °C, 90% humidity
  2. Pinning 6 days, 16 °C, 95% humidity
  3. Fruiting 10 days, 15 °C, 92% humidity
