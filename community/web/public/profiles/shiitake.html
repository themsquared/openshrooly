Culinary Profile

Shiitake

Shiitake fruits at 16 C and 85% RH with moderate FAE and 12 h light. About 74 days from start to fruit. Needs a full colonize + browning rest, then a cold shock before fruiting.

Lentinula edodes, Warm-weather block

Grow parameters

  1. Colonization 42 d, 88% RH, 24 C

    Wait for full white, then browning . do not fruit early.

  2. Browning / skin 21 d, 80% RH, 21 C

    Block forms a protective brown skin. Keep it a bit drier.

  3. Cold shock (off-device) 1 d, 90% RH, 16 C

    Firmware cannot reach fridge temps. Shock 12-24 h at 5-10 °C, then apply fruiting.

  4. Fruiting 10 d, 85% RH, 16 C
