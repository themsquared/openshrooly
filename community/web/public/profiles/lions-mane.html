Culinary Profile

Lion's Mane

Lion's Mane fruits at 17 C and 90% RH with moderate FAE and 10 h light. About 33 days from start to fruit. Tooth fungus. Moderate FAE — too much air yellows spines.

Hericium erinaceus, Standard

Grow parameters

  1. Colonization 16 d, 90% RH, 22 C

  2. Pinning 5 d, 94% RH, 18 C

    Look for dense white primordia, not fuzzy overlay.

  3. Fruiting 12 d, 90% RH, 17 C

    Harvest when spines elongate but before they yellow.
