Culinary Profile

Blue Oyster

Pleurotus ostreatus var. columbinus, Blue

Blue Oyster fruits at 14 °C and 87% humidity with plenty of fresh air and 12 h light. About 25 days from start to fruit.

Cool-preferring oyster. Deep color holds better below 18 °C.

Grow parameters

  1. Colonization 12 days, 22 °C, 90% humidity
  2. Pinning 4 days, 15 °C, 94% humidity

    Cooler pinning keeps blue tones.

  3. Fruiting 9 days, 14 °C, 87% humidity
