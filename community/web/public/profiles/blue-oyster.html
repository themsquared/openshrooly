Culinary Profile

Blue Oyster

Blue Oyster fruits at 14 C and 87% RH with high FAE and 12 h light. About 25 days from start to fruit. Cool-preferring oyster. Deep color holds better below 18 °C.

Pleurotus ostreatus var. columbinus, Blue

Grow parameters

  1. Colonization 12 d, 90% RH, 22 C

  2. Pinning 4 d, 94% RH, 15 C

    Cooler pinning keeps blue tones.

  3. Fruiting 9 d, 87% RH, 14 C
