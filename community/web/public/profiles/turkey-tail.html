How to grow Turkey Tail at home

Trametes versicolor, Wild-type

Updated 2026-10-26

To grow Turkey Tail at home, colonize at 24 °C (75 °F) in the dark, then fruit at 20 °C (68 °F) and 86% humidity with moderate fresh air and 12 h of light a day. This profile runs about 78 days from the start of colonization to the end of the first harvest window.

Thin polypore. Long fruiting, lots of fresh air, not a thick-fleshed edible.

What goes wrong when growing Turkey Tail?

No pins after colonization

Make sure the block is fully colonized first. Then check that the chamber reaches the primordia conditions: 21 °C (70 °F), 90% humidity, and moderate fresh air. Pins need more fresh air than colonization. More on this.

Green, black, or slimy patches

Patches that are not white mycelium are usually contamination such as green mold or bacteria. Bag the block, take it out of the chamber, and clean the chamber before the next grow. More on this.

Turkey Tail growing FAQ

What temperature does Turkey Tail grow at?

In this profile Turkey Tail colonizes at 24 °C (75 °F) and fruits at 20 °C (68 °F). Colonization: 24 °C (75 °F). Primordia: 21 °C (70 °F). Brackets: 20 °C (68 °F).

What humidity does Turkey Tail need?

86% while fruiting. Colonization: 88%. Primordia: 90%. Brackets: 86%.

How long does Turkey Tail take to grow?

About 78 days with this profile: 28 days of colonization, 10 days of primordia, 40 days of brackets. Real blocks vary, so move on when the block is ready, not only when the days run out.

How much light does Turkey Tail need?

Colonization: dark. Primordia: 12 h a day, white 20%, blue 80%. Brackets: 12 h a day, white 20%, blue 80%.

How much fresh air does Turkey Tail need?

Moderate fresh air: fan runs 30 s every 12 min while fruiting. Colonization runs with little fresh air.
