Culinary Profile

Turkey Tail

Turkey Tail fruits at 20 C and 86% RH with moderate FAE and 12 h light. About 78 days from start to fruit. Thin polypore. Long fruiting, lots of FAE, not a thick-fleshed edible.

Trametes versicolor, Wild-type

Grow parameters

  1. Colonization 28 d, 88% RH, 24 C

  2. Primordia 10 d, 90% RH, 21 C

  3. Brackets 40 d, 86% RH, 20 C
