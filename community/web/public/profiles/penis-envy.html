Penis Envy

Psilocybe cubensis, Penis Envy

Legality of cultivating Psilocybe species varies by jurisdiction. This pack is informational environmental setpoints for a fruiting chamber and is disabled by default. You are responsible for following local law.

Penis Envy fruits at 22 °C and 90% RH with moderate FAE and 12 h light. About 50 days from start to fruit.

Slower cubensis. Same climate band as Golden Teacher with longer stage timers.

Grow parameters

  1. Colonization 24 days, 25 °C, 92% RH
  2. Pinning 10 days, 22 °C, 95% RH
  3. Fruiting 16 days, 22 °C, 90% RH
