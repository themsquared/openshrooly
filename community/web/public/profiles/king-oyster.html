Culinary Profile

King Oyster

King Oyster fruits at 15 C and 82% RH with moderate FAE and 10 h light. About 38 days from start to fruit. Thick-stem oyster. Cooler, slightly drier, and less FAE than cluster oysters.

Pleurotus eryngii, King

Grow parameters

  1. Colonization 18 d, 88% RH, 22 C

  2. Pinning 6 d, 90% RH, 16 C

  3. Fruiting 14 d, 82% RH, 15 C

    Let stems elongate before caps flare if you want steak-cut kings.
