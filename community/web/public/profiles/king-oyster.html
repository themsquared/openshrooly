How to grow King Oyster at home

Pleurotus eryngii, King

Updated 2026-10-27

King Oyster (Pleurotus eryngii), also called king trumpet or French horn mushroom, takes about 38 days to grow at home, from spawn to the end of the first harvest. Colonize at 22 °C (72 °F) in the dark, then fruit at 15 °C (59 °F) and 82% humidity with moderate fresh air and 12 h of light a day.

Thick-stem oyster. Cooler, slightly drier, and less fresh air than cluster oysters.

Light recipe

Dark while it colonizes, then 12 h a day of cool white with some blue.

Light details

Same dark spawn run as other oysters. Fruiting is blue-heavy because P. eryngii primordia respond to blue light. Hours and white percent are the oyster default (12 h, 20% / 92 lux). No king-oyster-specific lux was found.

WS2812 blue is roughly 465 to 475 nm. The RGB mix is an approximation of cited wavelengths, not a measured spectrum.

What goes wrong when growing King Oyster?

What does this profile watch for?

High humidity plus lots of fresh air makes skinny kings. Keep humidity a bit lower once pins set.

No pins after colonization

Make sure the block is fully colonized first. Then check that the chamber reaches the pinning conditions: 16 °C (61 °F), 90% humidity, and moderate fresh air. Pins need more fresh air than colonization. More on this.

Dry, cracked, or curled caps

Usually humidity is too low, or air is blowing straight on the mushrooms. This profile holds 82% humidity while fruiting. More on this.

Green, black, or slimy patches

Patches that are not white mycelium are usually contamination such as green mold or bacteria. Bag the block, take it out of the chamber, and clean the chamber before the next grow. More on this.

Kit or block doing nothing? Work through grow kit not growing. To check your fresh air, put your chamber size and fan into the fresh air planner.

King Oyster growing FAQ

What temperature does King Oyster grow at?

In this profile King Oyster colonizes at 22 °C (72 °F) and fruits at 15 °C (59 °F). Colonization: 22 °C (72 °F). Pinning: 16 °C (61 °F). Fruiting: 15 °C (59 °F).

What humidity does King Oyster need?

82% while fruiting. Colonization: 88%. Pinning: 90%. Fruiting: 82%.

How long does King Oyster take to grow?

About 38 days with this profile: 18 days of colonization, 6 days of pinning, 14 days of fruiting. Real blocks vary, so move on when the block is ready, not only when the days run out.

How much light does King Oyster need?

Colonization: dark. Pinning: 12 h a day, white 20%, blue 80%. Fruiting: 12 h a day, white 20%, blue 80%.

How much fresh air does King Oyster need?

Moderate fresh air: fan runs 25 s every 25 min while fruiting. Colonization runs with little fresh air.
