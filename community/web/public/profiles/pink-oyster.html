Culinary Profile

Pink Oyster

Pink Oyster fruits at 24 C and 86% RH with high FAE and 12 h light. About 20 days from start to fruit. Tropical oyster. Wants warmth; stalls and fades in a cold room.

Pleurotus djamor, Pink

Grow parameters

  1. Colonization 10 d, 88% RH, 27 C

  2. Pinning 3 d, 92% RH, 24 C

  3. Fruiting 7 d, 86% RH, 24 C

    Color fades as fruits mature; harvest while still vivid.
