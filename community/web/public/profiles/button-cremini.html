Culinary Profile

Button / Cremini

Agaricus bisporus, Brown cremini

Button / Cremini fruits at 16 °C and 88% RH with moderate FAE and 2 h light. About 36 days from start to fruit.

Compost + casing species. Low light, cool fruiting, casing required.

Grow parameters

  1. Spawn run 16 days, 24 °C, 88% RH

    Colonize compost in the dark before casing.

  2. Casing / pinning 8 days, 17 °C, 92% RH

    Apply a non-nutritive casing, then drop temp.

  3. Fruiting 12 days, 16 °C, 88% RH
