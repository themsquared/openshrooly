Culinary Profile

Enoki

Flammulina velutipes, White enoki

Enoki fruits at 15 °C and 93% RH with low FAE and 4 h light. About 43 days from start to fruit.

Needs cold fruiting and low FAE for supermarket-long stems.

Grow parameters

  1. Colonization 18 days, 22 °C, 90% RH
  2. Pinning 7 days, 16 °C, 95% RH

    Keep still and wet to pile up pins.

  3. Fruiting 18 days, 15 °C, 93% RH

    Low FAE stretches stems. More air makes stocky wild-type caps.
