Culinary

Enoki

Needs cold fruiting and low FAE for supermarket-long stems.

Flammulina velutipes · White enoki

Grow parameters

  1. Colonization 18 d · 90% RH · 22 °C

  2. Pinning 7 d · 95% RH · 16 °C

    Keep still and wet to pile up pins.

  3. Fruiting 18 d · 93% RH · 15 °C

    Low FAE stretches stems. More air makes stocky wild-type caps.
