How to grow Enoki at home

Flammulina velutipes, White enoki

Updated 2026-10-26

To grow Enoki at home, colonize at 22 °C (72 °F) in the dark, then fruit at 15 °C (59 °F) and 93% humidity with little fresh air and no light. This profile runs about 43 days from the start of colonization to the end of the first harvest window.

Needs cold fruiting and little fresh air for supermarket-long stems.

Light recipe

No light needed. It grows in the dark.

Light details

Commercial long-stem enoki is grown with little light. Sakamoto 2004 showed a cold dark room makes long pale pinhead fruits; light then forms a cap. This recipe keeps both phases at 0 h so stems stay long. That is a morphology choice from the paper, not a lux number.

WS2812 blue is roughly 465 to 475 nm. The RGB mix is an approximation of cited wavelengths, not a measured spectrum.

What goes wrong when growing Enoki?

What does this profile watch for?

A Shrooly cannot reach true 4 to 10 °C enoki rooms. This profile uses the coolest firmware target (15 °C) and very little fresh air as a chamber approximation.

No pins after colonization

Make sure the block is fully colonized first. Then check that the chamber reaches the pinning conditions: 16 °C (61 °F), 95% humidity, and little fresh air. Pins need more fresh air than colonization. More on this.

Dry, cracked, or curled caps

Usually humidity is too low, or air is blowing straight on the mushrooms. This profile holds 93% humidity while fruiting. More on this.

Green, black, or slimy patches

Patches that are not white mycelium are usually contamination such as green mold or bacteria. Bag the block, take it out of the chamber, and clean the chamber before the next grow. More on this.

Enoki growing FAQ

What temperature does Enoki grow at?

In this profile Enoki colonizes at 22 °C (72 °F) and fruits at 15 °C (59 °F). Colonization: 22 °C (72 °F). Pinning: 16 °C (61 °F). Fruiting: 15 °C (59 °F).

What humidity does Enoki need?

93% while fruiting. Colonization: 90%. Pinning: 95%. Fruiting: 93%.

How long does Enoki take to grow?

About 43 days with this profile: 18 days of colonization, 7 days of pinning, 18 days of fruiting. Real blocks vary, so move on when the block is ready, not only when the days run out.

How much light does Enoki need?

Colonization: dark. Pinning: dark. Fruiting: dark.

How much fresh air does Enoki need?

Little fresh air: fan runs 15 s every 90 min while fruiting. Colonization runs with little fresh air.

Is Enoki good for beginners?

It is one of the harder species in this library. Try an oyster first if you have never grown before.

Sources
  • Sakamoto 2004 enoki. Yuichi Sakamoto, Influence of light on the morphological changes that take place during the development of the Flammulina velutipes fruit body, Mycoscience 45 (2004). A temperature drop induces fruiting in the dark. Dark fruits are long, pale, and pinhead. Light then forms a cap.
  • Sakamoto 2018. Yuichi Sakamoto, Influences of environmental factors on fruiting body induction, development and maturation in mushroom-forming fungi, Fungal Biology Reviews 32 (2018). Light is a fruiting cue for many basidiomycetes. Blue light is the usual photomorphogenesis signal. Agaricus bisporus is called out as not needing light for a cap. Flammulina velutipes can fruit in the dark as a long stem with a tiny cap.
  • Stamets. Paul Stamets, Growing Gourmet and Medicinal Mushrooms, Ten Speed Press. Used here as a general cultivation reference. This recipe does not take a photoperiod or lux number from a cited page in that book.
