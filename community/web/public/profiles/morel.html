How to grow Morel at home

Morchella importuna, Morel

Updated 2026-10-30

Morel (Morchella importuna) takes about 45 days to grow at home, from spawn to the end of the first harvest. Colonize at 20 °C (68 °F) in the dark, then fruit at 15 °C (59 °F) and 88% humidity with moderate fresh air and 12 h of light a day.

Prized spring mushroom. Usually grown outdoors in beds, so this is a best-effort chamber climate, not a proven indoor recipe.

Light recipe

Dark while it colonizes, then 12 h a day of cool white with some blue.

Light details

Morels are usually an outdoor bed crop. No indoor lux or photoperiod paper was found, so this is the labeled general default.

WS2812 blue is roughly 465 to 475 nm. The RGB mix is an approximation of cited wavelengths, not a measured spectrum.

What goes wrong when growing Morel?

What does this profile watch for?

Fruiting morels in a Shrooly or any small chamber is atypical and often fails. Most growers use outdoor or greenhouse beds with a nutrient bag. This card only sets humidity, temperature, light, and fresh air for people who want to try.

No pins after colonization

Make sure the block is fully colonized first. Then check that the chamber reaches the pinning conditions: 14 °C (57 °F), 92% humidity, and moderate fresh air. Pins need more fresh air than colonization. More on this.

Dry, cracked, or curled caps

Usually humidity is too low, or air is blowing straight on the mushrooms. This profile holds 88% humidity while fruiting. More on this.

Green, black, or slimy patches

Patches that are not white mycelium are usually contamination such as green mold or bacteria. Bag the block, take it out of the chamber, and clean the chamber before the next grow. More on this.

Kit or block doing nothing? Work through grow kit not growing. To check your fresh air, put your chamber size and fan into the fresh air planner.

Morel growing FAQ

What temperature does Morel grow at?

In this profile Morel colonizes at 20 °C (68 °F) and fruits at 15 °C (59 °F). Colonization: 20 °C (68 °F). Pinning: 14 °C (57 °F). Fruiting: 15 °C (59 °F).

What humidity does Morel need?

88% while fruiting. Colonization: 90%. Pinning: 92%. Fruiting: 88%.

How long does Morel take to grow?

About 45 days with this profile: 21 days of colonization, 10 days of pinning, 14 days of fruiting. Real blocks vary, so move on when the block is ready, not only when the days run out.

How much light does Morel need?

Colonization: dark. Pinning: 12 h a day, white 20%, blue 80%. Fruiting: 12 h a day, white 20%, blue 80%.

How much fresh air does Morel need?

Moderate fresh air: fan runs 30 s every 20 min while fruiting. Colonization runs with little fresh air.

Is Morel good for beginners?

It is one of the harder species in this library. Try an oyster first if you have never grown before.
