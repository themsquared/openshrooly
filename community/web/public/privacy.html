Privacy

What we keep, and why.

Last updated October 25, 2026.

Shroom Labs keeps the least it can to run your account, your chambers, and your grow alerts. We don't sell your data and we don't run ad trackers.

What we collect

  • Your account. Email, username, and a hashed password. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we get your email and an account id from them. Passkeys stay on your device; we only keep the public half.
  • Your chambers. If you link a chamber, it sends readings like temperature, humidity, light, and fresh air, plus its firmware version and the Profile it's running. Commands you send to it are logged.
  • What you post. Profiles you build, ratings, grow reports, and photos you upload.
  • Purchases. What you bought and when. Card details go straight to Stripe. We never see or store your card number.
  • Grow Coach. If you use it, your questions, the answers, and the chamber readings it looked at.
  • Security records. Sign-ins, new devices, and changes to your account, so we can spot trouble. Your IP address is used for a short time to stop spam and abuse.

What we don't do

  • No selling or renting your data.
  • No ad trackers or third party analytics on this site.
  • No emails you didn't ask for. Grow alerts are opt in, and every one has an unsubscribe link.

Cookies

One cookie keeps you signed in, and your browser remembers your sign-in for this site. That's it. No tracking cookies, so there's no cookie banner.

Who helps us run it

These companies handle data for us, only to run the service:

  • Cloudflare runs our servers, database, photo storage, and Grow Coach.
  • Netlify hosts this website.
  • Resend sends account and alert emails.
  • Stripe takes payments.
  • Google and Apple, only if you choose to sign in with them.

Your data is yours

  • Download it. Go to Account, Your data and tap Download my data. You get one file with everything above.
  • Delete it. Same place. Deleting your account removes your profile, posts, photos, alerts, and chamber history, and unlinks your chambers. They keep growing on your Wi-Fi.
  • Fix it. You can change most things in Account. For anything else, email us.

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete it, it's gone from our database right away. Backups roll off within 30 days. We keep payment records as long as tax law says we have to.

Kids

Shroom Labs is for adults. You need to be 18 or older to make an account.

Changes

If we change this page in a way that matters, we'll email account holders before it takes effect.

Questions

Get in touch. A real person reads it.
