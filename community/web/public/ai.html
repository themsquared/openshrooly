AI for growers

How Shroom Labs uses AI to grow mushrooms

By Mike Moore · Updated 2026-11-21

Shroom Labs uses AI in a few narrow places: Grow Coach compares a chamber's live readings with the grow profile for your species and recommends changes, Autopilot makes those changes in small steps inside limits written in code, and a photo check reads a picture of your grow. The target numbers come from researched grow profiles, not from a model, and the chamber firmware is open source and keeps growing on its own if the internet or our site goes down.

What does the AI actually do in a Shroom Labs chamber?

It watches, explains, and nudges: Grow Coach reads the chamber, Autopilot adjusts targets, and the photo check looks at your block.

Underneath all of it is open firmware that runs the mister, fans and light locally. AI only ever sends new targets to it.

What does the AI not do?

It does not invent targets, it does not run the chamber's basic control loop, and it does not identify wild mushrooms.

  • No made up numbers. Targets start from the grow profile for the species and stage you picked. Autopilot moves them one small fixed step at a time and code clamps the result. See the limits.
  • No hidden control. The chamber's own firmware holds humidity, fresh air and light. If our site is down, it keeps going on its last targets.
  • No species identification. The photo check assumes you know what you are growing. For wild mushrooms, read can AI identify mushrooms safely.
  • Culinary only. Grow Coach, Autopilot and the photo check refuse anything that is not a culinary grow.

Which chambers can use Grow Coach and Autopilot?

A Shrooly running OpenShrooly and a Lykyn running Shroom Labs firmware, once you link the chamber to your account.

Both run on real hardware and are marked supported on the chambers page. Terrashroom firmware and the AC Infinity adapter exist but are untested on real hardware, so only Shrooly and Lykyn are marked supported. Any other chamber, tub or tent can still use the free grow profiles by hand.

Do I need AI to grow mushrooms with Shroom Labs?

No. The grow profiles and firmware are free, and a chamber runs fine on its own without an account or Grow Coach.

There are 24 free culinary profiles, each with temperature, humidity, fresh air and light for every stage. Load one and the chamber follows it. Grow Coach adds a second set of eyes for people who want the chamber to adjust itself over a long grow. If you are new, start with can AI grow mushrooms for an honest look at what it adds.

What does it cost?

Grow Coach, which includes Autopilot and the photo check, is a paid monthly or yearly plan; the firmware, free profiles, charts and MCP server cost nothing.

You can cancel Grow Coach anytime from your account. Current prices are on the Grow Coach page.

How is the AI kept from making a bad change?

Every limit lives in code the model cannot change: small fixed steps, at most one change an hour and eight a day, hard temperature and humidity ranges, and an automatic pause when readings look stale or broken.

The full list, with the numbers, is on the Autopilot page. Each change also lands in a decisions feed with who made it and why, and you can undo it.

AI features

  • Grow Coach: per-species advice from your chamber's own readings Grow Coach compares your chamber's live readings with the culinary profile for your species, keeps track of how your chamber behaves, and explains each change.
  • Autopilot: how the chamber adjusts itself, and the limits it cannot cross Autopilot sets temperature, humidity, fresh air, light and stage from your mushroom profile in small logged steps, inside hard limits, with undo.
  • Photo check and readings charts: seeing what the chamber sees Snap a phone photo of your mushroom grow for an AI check against the chamber's readings, and read live charts with target bands and change markers.
  • Open firmware for Shrooly and Lykyn: AI on top, local control underneath OpenShrooly and Shroom Labs Lykyn firmware run humidity, fresh air and light locally. Grow Coach only sends targets over an optional, outbound cloud link.
  • Ask your AI assistant about mushrooms with the Shroom Labs MCP server A free, read only MCP server at api.shroomlabs.io/mcp lets AI assistants look up mushroom grow profiles, stage conditions, timelines and guides.
