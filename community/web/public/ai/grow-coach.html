Grow Coach: per-species advice from your chamber's own readings

By Mike Moore · Updated 2026-11-21

Grow Coach reads your chamber's live temperature, humidity and fan readings, compares them with the stage targets in the culinary profile you picked, and tells you what to change and why. It also keeps a running picture of how your particular chamber behaves, such as whether it lags its humidity target, and answers questions about your grow in plain words. It is a paid plan you can cancel anytime.

How does Grow Coach decide what to recommend?

It compares the latest reading with the profile's target band for the current stage and with your chamber's own track record over the last day, then writes a recommendation with the reason attached.

The track record is a small per chamber model built from recent readings. It is plain math, not a language model:

  • Humidity error and droop. How far humidity sits from its target on average, and whether it tends to fall short.
  • Temperature offset. How far the chamber runs warm or cool against its target.
  • Humidity rise rate. How fast humidity climbs, which shows whether the mister keeps up.
  • Water use and average fan speed. From the chamber's own reports.
  • Your grow outcomes. How often your past culinary grows on that chamber went well.

A useful detail: when humidity is off, Coach leaves the humidity target at the profile value and works on fresh air instead of chasing the humidity number up and down. A warm or cool reading outside the profile's temperature band gets a plain instruction, such as lowering the target back toward the profile value. Only a real sensor calibration value can shift the suggested temperature, and never outside the profile's band. Source: Grow Coach chamber model and recommendation rules.

Why does each species get different advice?

Because every culinary profile has its own targets for each stage, and Grow Coach measures your chamber against the profile you picked, not a generic number.

What alerts does Grow Coach send?

Three grow alerts: humidity trouble when it misses the target by 8 points or more on average, colonization that looks stalled, and a heads up when the fruiting window is nearly over.

  • Humidity trouble. Humidity misses the target by a wide margin. The message tells you to check the reservoir and the door seal.
  • Stalled colonization. More than 70% of the colonization stage has passed, humidity sits 5 or more points under its target, and it is not climbing.
  • Time to harvest. 90% of the fruiting stage has passed. Check cap size and harvest if veils are thinning.

Account alerts for water level and an offline chamber are separate and work without Grow Coach. See the alert rules.

Can I ask Grow Coach questions about my grow?

Yes. The chat answers in plain prose using your chamber's readings, its learned model, and the stage targets of the profile you selected.

Because it sees the same numbers you do, a question like "why are my pins drying out" gets an answer that mentions your actual humidity, not a textbook range. There is a daily limit on chats and photo checks so costs stay predictable. Questions about anything that is not a culinary grow are refused.

What will Grow Coach never do?

It never suggests humidity outside 70 to 96% or temperature outside 10 to 32 °C, never moves more than 6 points of humidity or 2 °C from the profile, and never coaches anything but a culinary grow.

Per stage warning bands in a profile tighten that range further. The bounds are in the Coach source, which is public. Grow Coach also does not identify species: it assumes you know what you are growing because you picked the profile.

Should I use Advisor or Autopilot?

Start with Advisor, where you approve each change with one tap, and switch to Autopilot once the reasons it gives make sense to you.

Autopilot makes the same kind of changes on its own, with extra limits on how often and how far. Read how Autopilot works first. You can switch back to Advisor anytime.

What do I need to use Grow Coach?

A Shrooly running OpenShrooly or a Lykyn running Shroom Labs firmware, linked to a Shroom Labs account, plus a Grow Coach plan.

Install OpenShrooly from the install page or read Lykyn for mushrooms, then link the chamber. The chamber reports in about every 15 minutes, and that is what Coach reads.
