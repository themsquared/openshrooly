Autopilot: how the chamber adjusts itself, and the limits it cannot cross

By Mike Moore · Updated 2026-11-21

Autopilot is the hands off mode of Grow Coach. It picks the profile stage from your grow day, sets fresh air and light from that stage's recipe, and nudges the temperature target, humidity target and fan speed in small steps, never more than one change an hour or 8 a day. Every change lands in a decisions feed with who decided and why, and you can undo it.

What can Autopilot change?

Temperature target, humidity target, fan speed, the fresh air schedule, the light schedule, light brightness and color, and the grow stage, but only the ones your chamber says it supports.

Chambers report a list of what they can take. Autopilot never sends a setting a chamber did not list, and older firmware that only takes temperature and humidity just gets those. Fresh air timing and the light schedule come straight from the stage recipe in the profile. Fan speed may move up to 20 points from the recipe in steps of 10, always inside 0 to 80%. Light brightness stays within 50% of the recipe. Source: Autopilot stage, fan, fresh air and light rules.

How does Autopilot know which stage the grow is in?

It counts grow days through the profile's stage lengths, so a profile with a 16 day colonization moves to pinning on day 17, and a stage you pick yourself always wins.

On chambers that report their phase, Autopilot can also move the chamber to the next stage when the day count says it is time.

What limits are hard coded?

Fixed ranges, fixed step sizes, and a fixed pace that the model cannot change.

Where does the AI come in?

On each pass a small decision model answers five narrow questions: raise, hold or lower for temperature, humidity, fan and light, plus whether the readings look broken.

The model sees the chamber's structured state only: profile, stage, grow day, the last 6 hours of readings in 30 minute buckets, current targets, the guardrails, and its own last few changes. It sees no free text and no personal data. Then code takes over:

  • A raise or lower only counts at a confidence of 0.6 or higher. Below that, the plain rules decide that knob.
  • A confident answer moves exactly one fixed step: 0.5 °C, 2 points of humidity, 10 points of fan, or 50 units of white light.
  • The result still goes through every limit in the table above.
  • If the model is slow, errors, or answers in the wrong shape, that pass uses the rules.

Hold is always the safe answer. Source: Autopilot decision model: questions, confidence and step sizes.

How does Autopilot handle humidity and fresh air together?

It holds the fan step until humidity is within 1 point of target, and only reaches further when humidity stays off target for an hour without improving.

Every fan run pulls moist air out, so a fan change can fight the mister. When humidity stays out of band and is not moving toward the target, Autopilot may step the fan up to 30 points from the recipe, still inside the fan range. More on the physics in how a smart grow box automates humidity and fresh air.

When does Autopilot pause itself?

When the chamber looks offline, readings are missing or older than 20 minutes, a reading is physically impossible, or a reading jumps more than 8 °C or 25 points of humidity from the last one.

Physically impossible means outside 0 to 45 °C or 10 to 100% humidity, which usually means a sensor problem. A short quiet spell just makes it wait. A bad sensor reading switches it back to Advisor. The decision model's broken readings question can do the same: at 0.85 or above, Autopilot drops to Advisor, sends nothing to the chamber, and emails you if email alerts are on.

What is the decisions feed?

A newest first list of what Coach changed, who decided, and why, with an undo for each change.

The same decisions show as markers on the chamber's readings chart, so you can see a humidity dip and the change that followed it on one screen. See the readings charts.

Can I still change settings by hand?

Not while Autopilot is on: the site locks climate, fresh air, light, stage and what is growing until you switch Coach back to Advisor.

That keeps two drivers from fighting over one chamber. The message you see is "Coach is managing targets. Turn Coach off to set them yourself."
