AI grow coach vs checking your grow by hand

By Mike Moore · Updated 2026-11-21

Checking by hand works fine for a kit on the counter: look twice a day, mist, fan. An AI coach pays off when a chamber runs for weeks: it reads every check in, spots slow drift like a chamber that always lags its humidity target, and leaves a written reason for each change. It does not replace your eyes on the block; contamination and harvest timing still need a look.

How do the two compare?

A coach wins on how often it looks and on keeping records; you win on everything you can see and touch.

When is checking by hand enough?

For one kit at a time on a counter or in a simple tub, where misting and a look twice a day cover it.

Use the grow timeline calculator to know what to expect when, and the profile for your species for the numbers.

When does a coach help most?

On a chamber that runs unattended for weeks, slow species with long stages, and chambers that cannot quite hold their targets.

The chamber model shows patterns a daily glance misses, such as humidity that sags a few points every afternoon. Coach also flags colonization that looks stalled and tells you when the fruiting window is nearly over.

What can a coach miss?

Anything the sensor cannot measure: mold, pests, a dry block surface under good air readings, or a reservoir that is about to run dry on a chamber with no tank sensor.

That is why the photo check exists, and why it reports a confidence score rather than a verdict.

Can I use both?

Yes, and Advisor mode is built for that: Coach recommends, you look at the block, and you approve the changes that match what you see.

Switch to Autopilot later if you find yourself approving everything.
