How does a smart mushroom grow box automate humidity and fresh air?

By Mike Moore · Updated 2026-11-21

A smart grow box runs a loop. A sensor reads humidity, the mister switches on when humidity drops below the target minus a small margin and off when it passes the target plus that margin, and a fan runs on a timer, a set number of seconds every so many minutes, to swap stale air for fresh. The grow profile sets those numbers for each species and stage, and a coach layer like Autopilot can shift them when the chamber drifts.

How does the humidity loop work?

The profile sets a target and a margin, called hysteresis, and the mister runs only while humidity is below the bottom of that band.

Chestnut Mushroom92%below 90%, off above 94%30 s every 15 min

The margin stops the mister from clicking on and off every few seconds. A lower humidity target or a wider margin means drier swings between mist bursts.

How does fresh air exchange work in a small chamber?

A fan runs for a set number of seconds every set number of minutes at a set speed, which flushes out the carbon dioxide the mushrooms breathe out.

Too little fresh air gives long stems and small caps. Too much dries the surface. To size a fan cycle for your own box or tent, use the fresh air planner, and see leggy mushroom stems for the symptoms.

Why do humidity and fresh air fight each other?

Every fan run pulls moist air out, so more fresh air means the mister has to work harder to hold the same humidity.

That is why Autopilot holds a fan step until humidity is within 1 point of target, and only reaches further, up to 30 points from the recipe, when humidity stays off target for an hour without improving. A person tends to over correct here; a slow, spaced rule does not.

What happens when the water runs out?

Shroom Labs can email a low water alert at 30% and an urgent empty alert at 12% for chambers that report a water level.

The empty alert sends even during quiet hours. Firmware that also stops the mister on an empty tank, and a version for chambers with no tank sensor that infers an empty tank by watching humidity fail to rise while the mister runs, are in progress and not released yet. See the alert rules.

What about temperature?

It depends on the chamber: a Lykyn has no heater or cooler, so it tracks temperature and boosts the fans when it runs hot, and the room has to do the rest.

Most culinary species fruit in normal room temperatures, which is why small grow boxes skip a heater. Each profile lists its own range, for example Lion's Mane fruits at 17 °C (63 °F).

Can I automate a monotub or grow tent the same way?

Yes: a humidifier on a humidity controller and an exhaust fan on a cycle timer give you the same loop, set from the same profile numbers.

The AC Infinity build guide turns profile numbers into controller settings. Our AC Infinity adapter that would set them for you exists but is untested on real hardware. A Shrooly or Lykyn already has the loop built in: flash a Shrooly or flash a Lykyn and it runs from the same profiles, and Autopilot can tune it.
