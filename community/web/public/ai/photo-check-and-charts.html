Photo check and readings charts: seeing what the chamber sees

By Mike Moore · Updated 2026-11-21

There is no camera inside a Shroom Labs chamber. You take a photo with your phone, and Grow Coach's photo check reads it next to the chamber's readings, then names the stage it sees, any issues and how sure it is, and two to four next steps. The readings charts show temperature, humidity, fan and water over 6h, 24h, 7d, with the stage's target band and a marker for every Autopilot or Coach change.

Does the chamber have a built in camera?

No. Shrooly and Lykyn chambers have no camera we use, so the photo check works from a photo you take with your phone.

A Terrashroom has a camera module on its board, but our community Terrashroom firmware does not support it yet. A phone photo is sharper anyway, and you choose the angle.

What does the photo check tell you?

A one or two sentence summary, the stage it sees, any issues with a severity, two to four next steps, and a confidence score.

What a photo check returns
FieldWhat it holds
StageColonizing, pinning, fruiting, ready to harvest, past harvest, or unclear
IssuesEach with a short name, one sentence, and a severity of low, medium or high
Next stepsTwo to four short steps
Confidence0 to 1, so you know how much weight to give it

If you pick a chamber, the check also sees its stage, grow day, targets and recent readings, and says so plainly when readings are off target. On possible mold it is told to say what it sees and how sure it is rather than guess. For a closer look at mold, read mushroom contamination. Source: Grow Coach photo check.

What happens to my photo?

It is not saved: location and camera details are stripped, the image is checked once, and then it is dropped.

Photos that do not look like a mushroom grow get a polite "try a closer shot" instead of advice, and grows that are not culinary are refused. If you want to show off a harvest, post it on the grow feed instead, where you choose to make it public.

What is on the readings chart?

Stacked panels on one time axis for each reading the chamber sends, the current stage's target band, a line for each target you set, gaps where the chamber was offline, and lettered markers for changes.

How do the charts help find a problem?

Look for a reading that sits outside its shaded target band while the target you set is right, because that means the chamber cannot keep up rather than being set wrong.

  • Humidity under the band while its target is right. Check the reservoir and the door seal before changing anything.
  • A gap in the lines. The chamber stopped reporting. Autopilot pauses during gaps.
  • A marker right before a change in the line. Hover it to see whether Autopilot, a Coach suggestion, a profile or stage pick, or a firmware update caused it.

Can the photo check identify a wild mushroom?

No. It is built for a culinary grow you already know the species of, and it should never be used to decide whether a wild mushroom is safe to eat.

Read can AI identify mushrooms safely for what the research says about identification apps.
