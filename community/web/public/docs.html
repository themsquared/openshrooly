How the pieces fit.

Shroom Labs is a library of researched mushroom grow profiles. Apply them from a Hub to a chamber running OpenShrooly.

Profiles

A Profile is a chamber recipe: humidity, temperature, fresh air, light, and days for each stage. Browse Profiles on this site, then pull one to your Hub.

Hub

Hub is the local app on your network. It stores Profiles and sends each stage's temperature, humidity, light, and fresh air settings to a chamber. Growing does not need the cloud.

Grow Coach

Grow Coach is a culinary subscription, coming soon at $3.99 per month. It will use your device's readings and your past grows to tailor humidity and temperature advice. Photo checks come later.

Community

Community is this site: the public catalog, ratings, grow reports, and forks. Works with Shrooly devices running OpenShrooly.

Claim a chamber

Optional. The device shows a short code. Claiming binds it to your account for cloud tools. Local controls still win if the cloud is down.

Install and updates

Put OpenShrooly on a Shrooly from Chrome or Edge with a USB-C cable on the install page. Later versions install over Wi-Fi when you press Check for updates on your Shrooly's page. Only releases signed by Shroom Labs are accepted, and your Shrooly keeps its current version if anything looks wrong.
