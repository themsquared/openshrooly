How Grow Coach works

  1. Install OpenShrooly on your Shrooly. It runs on your Wi-Fi and keeps working without an account. Install guide
  2. Link the chamber to Shroom Labs. Coach needs the chamber's readings to know what is happening inside it. Link a chamber
  3. Pick a culinary Profile. Coach compares the readings with the Profile's stage targets and recommends changes when humidity or temperature drift.
  4. Choose how much control to hand over. In Advisor mode you approve each change. With Autopilot, Coach adjusts the targets itself and logs every decision with the reason.

Every change stays inside the Profile's safe range and you can undo it. Grow Coach is $4.99 a month or $49 a year. Cancel anytime.
