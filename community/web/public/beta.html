Join the Shroom Labs beta Beta

If your Shrooly sits unused because the app stopped working, this beta puts it back to work. It is free. Spots are limited to the first 50 linked chambers.

Who it is for

Shrooly owners with a chamber that still powers on, and a computer with Chrome or Edge for the install.

What you get

  • Free OpenShrooly firmware on your chamber. Signed releases. Wi-Fi updates roll back on their own if something goes wrong.
  • Your chamber on your phone. The Shroom Labs dashboard shows temperature, humidity and grow stage.
  • Free grow profiles for oyster, lion's mane, shiitake and more, stage by stage.
  • Control from anywhere: fresh air, light, and which stage the chamber is in.
  • Link this device from the chamber menu. Scan a QR code, nothing to type.
  • A direct line. Send feedback from your account. A person reads every message.

No account needed to grow. The chamber also runs on your own Wi-Fi with its own page.

What we ask

Use it, then tell us what broke, what confused you, and what you want next. In your account, open Feedback.

Join in three steps

  1. Install. On a computer with Chrome or Edge, plug the chamber in with a USB-C data cable and open the install page. About 5 minutes. A phone cannot do this step.
  2. Link. On the chamber, press button 4, choose Link this device, and scan the QR code with your phone.
  3. Done. Your chamber shows up in your account. Pick a profile and grow.
Install on your Shrooly

Before you start

  • It is a beta. Some things will break. We fix them fast, and your feedback decides what comes first.
  • Back up first. The installer does not save the original Shrooly software for you. The recovery guide shows how to save it over USB in about 10 minutes.
  • You can go back. With that backup you can flash the original software back at any time. Without one, you can always reinstall OpenShrooly over USB.
  • Screen stuck after install? Unplug USB and power, wait 10 seconds, then plug in power only.

Bonus: Shroomy the mushroom dances on the screen while your chamber grows.

Beta full?

When the 50 spots are taken, linking shows a waitlist. Leave your email and we will tell you when a spot opens. Your chamber still works on your own Wi-Fi in the meantime.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. The firmware, the dashboard, and the grow profiles are free in the beta.

Can I install from my phone?

No. The install needs a computer with Chrome or Edge and a USB-C data cable. Linking happens on your phone.

Do I need an account?

Not to grow. An account adds the dashboard, your chamber on your phone, and email alerts.

Who makes this?

Shroom Labs builds on OpenShrooly, the open source firmware started by Graham Stewart (grahamsz). We are not made by or affiliated with the company behind Shrooly.

Something went wrong. Where do I get help?

Start with the Shrooly FAQ and the recovery guide. Once your chamber is linked, send feedback and we will help.
