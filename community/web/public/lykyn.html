Lykyn for mushrooms

Updated 2026-11-13

A Lykyn Smart Mushroom Grow Box is a countertop chamber with an official app. Shroom Labs drives it without flashing, through a Socket.IO stand-in that speaks the same protocol as Lykyn's cloud. The box is on the bench with a Black Oyster bag. Live LAN control still needs a TLS cert the stock box trusts for lykyn.app, or a logged-in cloud session.

Does Shroom Labs work with Lykyn?

In progress. The box is on the bench with stock firmware, and a public Socket.IO stand-in speaks its cloud protocol. A live LAN push still needs a TLS cert the stock box trusts for lykyn.app, or a logged-in cloud session.

We leave the official firmware on the box. The Shroom Labs Lykyn adapter on GitHub accepts telemetry and pushes settings. Status is also on the grow log.

What is a Lykyn Smart Mushroom Grow Box?

A countertop smart chamber with fans, a humidifier, lights, and a temp and humidity sensor, set up from an official app over Bluetooth and run day to day through Lykyn's cloud.

Lykyn sells a Single-Tier box and a two layer model. The company says you add water, put in a fruiting block, cut an X, and pick the species in the app (Lykyn Smart Mushroom Grow Box). Our unit is a Single-Tier MUSHROOM box on firmware 0.17.0.

How does outside control work?

The box talks Socket.IO to lykyn.app. Our stand-in speaks the same events: it reads updateDevice telemetry and pushes settings the firmware applies.

The stand-in, CLI, and Black Oyster example profiles are in the Shroom Labs Lykyn adapter on GitHub. Credentials and device ids stay in a local .env and never go in git.

What still blocks a live LAN push?

TLS. The stock box verifies certificates for the hostname lykyn.app. A DNS override alone is not enough, and we will not reconfigure a home router until a trusted TLS path exists. A live LAN settings push is still untested on real hardware.

Two paths remain: terminate TLS in a way the stock box trusts, or drive settings through the owner's logged-in Lykyn cloud session. Details are in the adapter's dns-override doc.

How is this different from OpenShrooly?

OpenShrooly replaces the stock software on a Shrooly chamber by flashing it. The Lykyn path leaves the stock software on the box and talks to it from outside.

Flashing is how we run a Shrooly chamber, and it is the untested plan for a Terrashroom. Lykyn is the other path: keep the official firmware and build an adapter around its cloud protocol. Read the outside-control guide for the full split.

Can I help?

Yes, if you own a Lykyn. Message us with the model, Single-Tier or two layer, and what the official app lets you set.

Message us and pick A chamber. We are not asking anyone to flash a Lykyn. Notes about what the app shows, and whether it works without the cloud, all help. Every report goes into the adapter notes.
